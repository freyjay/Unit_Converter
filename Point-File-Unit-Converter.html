What this tool does
  • Convert point-file units between metres, international feet and U.S. survey feet using exact ratios.
  • Preserve your data: keep the source untouched and everything outside the selected coordinate values unchanged.
  • Check the result: verify every coordinate and refuse output that fails the arithmetic or preservation checks.
  • Keep the evidence: save a report or complete HTML handoff, and re-check your downloaded point file.

You confirm the units (including international vs U.S. survey foot) and column mapping. An independently known control point can catch a wrong declaration. Checks do not certify the survey or transform its coordinate system.

STEP 3Declare the units

  

    

    

      

      

    

    

    

    

  

  
Which foot?International foot = 0.3048 m exactly; US survey foot = 1200/3937 m. They differ by 2 ppm (about 2 ft on a 1,000,000 ft coordinate), and the file cannot tell you which it is: the choice is yours and is recorded as declared. To check it, enter an independently known point under Control points below with the tolerance its source justifies. A wrong choice is refused only when the difference it causes exceeds that tolerance, so a point near zero or a loose tolerance may not tell the two feet apart.

What a pass means, what it does not, and the limits
  

    
T1 · Source protectionThe source is never written. Its hash is recorded; the hash proves which bytes were converted, not their meaning.

    
T2 · Explicit meaningFormat, delimiter, header policy and units are declared by you and recorded. Detection only suggests.

    
T3 · Exact calculationSource numerals and unit factors are exact rationals. No binary floating point in the coordinate arithmetic.

    
T4 · Explicit roundingNearest, ties to even. The verifier recomputes every product with a second rounding procedure and requires the written token to equal it, to reverse within half the source’s last digit, and to reconstruct the source at its own quantum.

    
T5 · Complete accountingEvery line is blank, comment (#), header, or a valid data row. Any other line aborts the run with its line number.

    
T6 · PreservationEvery byte outside the coordinate fields is unchanged. Invalid UTF-8, a stray U+FEFF and NUL bytes are refused.

    
T7 · Independent verificationA separate verifier re-parses source and candidate before a download is offered; a complete handoff re-verifies its embedded bytes every time it is opened.

    
T8 · Bound provenanceThe report and manifest carry both hashes, the tool build, every setting, and a content fingerprint over all of them. A settings change invalidates the run.

    
T9 · Scope honestyA pass establishes arithmetic fidelity and preservation. It does not establish which foot the survey used, its datum, or that this file belongs to your site.

    
LimitsUTF-8/ASCII; 64 MiB source in this browser tool (the command-line engine takes 256 MB); 64 columns; 0–12 written decimals; numeric tokens of 40 digits and exponents within ±30; whitespace means ASCII space, tab, VT, FF; identifiers may be any text. Uniform scaling about zero only: no datum, projection, offset or per-axis factors.

    
PrivacyNo network: the page’s content policy blocks every connection. A complete handoff embeds the original source; share it only with people who should have that data. A handoff contains this app’s code; open only copies from a source you trust.
